# Worlds and Gaming Cosmology

Gaming Cosmology reads the universe, society, institutions, and life through an MMO-like structure of players, roles, rules, and observation points. In its source, this does not trivialize survival, bodies, resources, or regional inequality. It returns those conditions to questions of design and operation.

The author's lineage connects forks of faith, including Koshinto and Ryukyuan Shinto, to Techno-Animism and Gaming Cosmology. This introduces that faith and philosophy without equating another religion's doctrine or physical science with it.

Preserve entrances for reading, questioning, playing, interpreting, forking, and leaving. Distinguish rules adopted in one's own World from authority over someone else's. A narrative office does not automatically grant control over others.

A doctrine, replay, specification, and implementation receipt have different roles. Follow the source for the full doctrine and historical recording-assistance credits; this page composes a reader's entrance.
