# Begin with paper and a pen

An introduction can begin with one session rather than a demand to believe. Use paper and a pen to describe a World, participant roles, purpose, and an event to explore.

1. Record experiences, questions, and wishes in your own language.
2. Ask about the other's World and context without reducing their interpretation to a correct or incorrect answer.
3. Place interpretations beside an event while retaining the original record.
4. Preserve ways to stop, leave, or fork when a session does not fit.
5. Carry only material participants choose to share, with authorship and provenance.

This is an edited entrance to the source's TRPG session and replay practice. It does not establish a new rite, doctrine, medical effect, or reproduction protocol for an identical spiritual experience.

A fragment someone wants to test technically can move to a research or implementation workbench with explicit purposes and observation conditions. Participation does not require moving faith or narrative there.
