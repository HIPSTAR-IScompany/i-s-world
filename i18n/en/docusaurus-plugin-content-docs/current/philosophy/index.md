# Design that preserves meaning

What deserves to be selected as information? Bring meaning, beauty, awe, faith, identity, intention, subjectivity, ritual, narrative, and culture into design rather than leaving them as hidden assumptions.

Spiritual Engineering provides the philosophy, Infoton Engineering the research and engineering domain, and Context Engineering one implementation technique. Preserve what cannot be measured instead of discarding it as worthless. Record shareable observations separately from first-person experience.

For images, compare the author's willingness to use an output alongside count and pixel quality. For music, compare the desire to listen again alongside separation and loudness. Check whether a metric improved while the original purpose was lost.

Religion, theology, science, engineering, and art do not automatically become supreme judges over each other. Connect different meanings through separate presentations while preserving sources.
